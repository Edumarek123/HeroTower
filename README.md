# HeroTower

A game-development project exploring action-oriented multiplayer and cooperative game modes, with a fantasy combat system and a range of planned arenas and environments.

## Game concept

The project notes describe competitive PvP modes and cooperative PvE survival, supported by character equipment, weapons, spells, attributes, and combat systems.

## Planned game modes

### PvP
- Battle Royale
- 5v5 Boss Battle
- 2v2 Arena
- 1v1 Arena

### PvE
- Invasion Survival, with escalating waves and periodic boss encounters

The repository's planning notes also describe ranked play, leaderboards, championships, and esports-oriented competition.

## Gameplay systems under design

- Weapon and spell systems
- Armor categories
- Character attributes and derived combat values
- Physical and magical damage calculations
- Character actions, attacks, and defensive mechanics
- Monsters and enemy races

## Art direction and assets

The project notes outline character animations, modular equipment sprites, weapons, spells, armor, and a variety of environments, including castles, forests, dungeons, mountains, and caves.
